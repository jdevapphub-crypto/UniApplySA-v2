📘 Application Guide – documents & important notes

📄 Required documents (usually)

  • Certified copy of ID (or passport)
  • National Senior Certificate (NSC) / Grade 12 results
  • Academic record (if transferring)
  • Proof of residence
  • Proof of income (parents/guardian) for NSFAS
  • Testimonial / conduct letter (sometimes)

⚠️ Pay attention to

  • • Closing dates – universities: 30 Sept (varies); TVET: 30 Sept (Jan intake)
  • • APS score – meet minimum requirements for your chosen course
  • • NSFAS – apply early (usually opens Sept–Jan)
  • • Online applications – most institutions have online portals
  • • Application fee – some universities charge R100–R300
  • • Email & WhatsApp – keep checking for updates
